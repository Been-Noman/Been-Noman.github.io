About

Academic Profile

I hold a B.Sc. in Nuclear Engineering from the University of Dhaka. My academic interests are centered on quantum technologies and computational methods for nuclear physics and engineering, including quantum optimization, quantum simulation, quantum material science, quantum thermodynamics, plasma physics, and fusion engineering.

I am especially interested in problems where physical modeling, quantum-inspired search, and machine learning can jointly improve the efficiency of complex engineering design and simulation workflows.

Thesis

Undergrad Thesis

Bachelor's thesis submitted to the Department of Nuclear Engineering, University of Dhaka.

This thesis presents a hybrid computational framework for finding the best arrangement of fuel assemblies inside a nuclear reactor — a problem so complex that traditional methods struggle to solve it efficiently. By combining quantum-inspired search algorithms with a fast deep learning surrogate model and an evolutionary optimizer, the method identifies safe, high-performing fuel patterns in a fraction of the time required by conventional simulation-heavy approaches.
