Quantum Computing

Quantum Galton Board

Built Qiskit-based quantum circuits for normal, exponential, and Hadamard walk distributions inspired by the Universal Statistical Simulator framework. The implementation uses Pauli-X initialization, amplitude control, CSWAP routing, ancilla-assisted diffusion, noisy backend modeling, and transpilation optimization.

Reported high fidelities for smaller circuits and robust distribution alignment under IBM FakeGeneva noise models.

NDT / Nuclear Engineering

Eddy Current Testing

Trained at the NDT division of the Atomic Energy Centre, Dhaka, applying electromagnetic testing principles for material sorting, coating thickness measurement, and surface or near-surface discontinuity detection.

NDT / Materials

Magnetic Particle Testing

Studied magnetization methods and ferromagnetic defect detection techniques with an emphasis on the links between magnetism, metallurgy, and materials integrity assessment.

NDT / Radiation

Radiographic Testing of Welded Metal

Used film-based and digital radiographic techniques with X-ray and gamma-ray sources to identify internal discontinuities and inspect welded materials according to engineering standards.

NDT / Ultrasound

Ultrasonic Testing

Worked with ultrasound transmission, reflection, piezoelectric transducers, couplants, and material science principles for detecting defects in steel, concrete, and other structural materials.
