QWorld QIntern 2026 | Research Internship

Learning-Guided Graph Coarsening for the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem

Developed a PyTorch graph neural network to guide customer aggregation in the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP), reducing problem size while preserving vehicle-capacity constraints.

Integrated classical and QUBO-based optimization solvers with route reconstruction and cross-cluster variable neighborhood search. Evaluated the framework on 14 benchmark instances using source-held-out validation and controlled ablation experiments.

Achieved a mean 4.38% route-cost reduction through cross-cluster refinement of GNN-coarsened solutions. All 42 runs across three random seeds satisfied vehicle-capacity and customer-coverage constraints.

WISER 2025 | Official Industry Track

Quantum Galton Board Simulator

Developed a quantum Galton board simulator in Qiskit using the Carney--Varcoe Universal Statistical Simulator framework. Implemented normal, exponential, and Hadamard quantum-walk distributions using quantum circuit-based probability generation.

Designed ancilla-assisted and CSWAP-based quantum circuits, incorporating controlled routing and quantum-walk operations. Evaluated simulation fidelity under IBM hardware noise models using conventional and AI-assisted transpilation techniques.

Successfully completed the assessed WISER industry-track project and earned official project-track certification.

Nuclear Engineering & NDT

Technical training at the Bangladesh Atomic Energy Commission, covering nondestructive evaluation, nuclear material characterization, and engineering inspection techniques.

NDT | Electromagnetic Testing

Eddy Current Testing

Received practical training in electromagnetic nondestructive evaluation at the Atomic Energy Centre, Dhaka. Studied eddy current responses for material sorting, coating thickness measurement, and the detection of surface and near-surface discontinuities.

NDT | Magnetic Inspection

Magnetic Particle Testing

Studied magnetization techniques and magnetic particle inspection for identifying surface and near-surface defects in ferromagnetic materials, with emphasis on material properties, magnetic flux leakage, and engineering integrity assessment.

NDT | Radiation-Based Inspection

Radiographic Testing of Welded Metal

Received training in radiographic inspection of welded materials using X-ray and gamma-ray imaging principles. Studied film-based and digital radiography, internal discontinuity identification, and radiation-based material inspection.

NDT | Ultrasonic Inspection

Ultrasonic Testing

Studied ultrasonic wave propagation, reflection, and transmission for nondestructive material evaluation. Worked with piezoelectric transducers, couplants, and ultrasonic defect-detection principles for assessing structural materials and engineering components.
