Nuclear Reactor Optimization

PWR Loading Pattern Optimization Using a Convolutional Neural Network Surrogate with Conformal Safety Calibration and Annealing-Initialized Genetic Search

  • Developed an active-learning CNN surrogate for static two-dimensional, 193-assembly pressurized water reactor fuel loading optimization, integrating targeted OpenMC evaluations, QUBO annealing warm starts, and constraint-preserving genetic search.
  • Incorporated strict out-of-fold residual modeling and one-sided conformal safety calibration, achieving 96.39% joint safety coverage across 360 prospective OpenMC evaluations.
  • Verified selected loading patterns against physical constraints using high-fidelity OpenMC Monte Carlo neutron transport calculations.
  • Conducted controlled statistical comparisons showing that local memetic refinement was the primary driver of objective improvement, while global search strategy and classical versus simulated quantum annealing initialization had comparatively small effects.

Nuclear Fusion & Materials

Neutron-Damage-Dependent Optimization of Thermal Detritiation in Actively Cooled W/Cu/CuCrZr Monoblocks

  • Investigated neutron-damage effects on tritium retention, trap evolution, and thermal release using coupled hydrogen-isotope transport and trapping simulations in FESTIM.
  • Studied the dependence of tritium removal on neutron damage, bake temperature, and bake duration in actively cooled tungsten-based plasma-facing components.
  • Developed an inventory-balanced operating map describing residual, vacuum-release, and coolant-transfer fractions to assess detritiation performance and competing coolant-transfer effects.

Quantum-Inspired & Combinatorial Optimization

LP-Guided Hierarchical QUBO Compression for the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows

  • Developed an LP-guided hierarchical QUBO framework for CVRPTW, combining dual-informed route generation with QUBO-based route-pool compression.
  • Integrated MILP-assisted route selection, Large Neighborhood Search, and exact refinement to minimize fleet size and travel distance under capacity and time-window constraints.
  • Evaluated 12 Solomon benchmark instances, achieving 87.88% average route-pool compression while retaining feasible routing solutions.
