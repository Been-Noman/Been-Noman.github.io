Current Research

Simulated Quantum Annealing–Genetic Algorithm Framework for Nuclear Fuel Loading Optimization

  • Developed a three-layer hybrid framework integrating quantum-inspired metaheuristics, deep-learning surrogate modeling, and evolutionary search for PWR fuel loading optimization.
  • Formulated multi-constraint fuel reloading placement as a 579-variable QUBO problem using PyQUBO and Simulated Quantum Annealing.
  • Trained a multi-output residual CNN surrogate with positional encoding and squeeze-and-excitation attention on 10,000 OpenMC Monte Carlo simulations.
  • Accelerated GA-based fitness evaluation from approximately 12 hours to under a second while targeting reduced peak power factors.

Current Research

QUBO-Based Benchmarking of Simulated Quantum and Classical Metaheuristics for PWR Fuel Loading Pattern Optimization

  • Extended a previous QUBO-based nuclear fuel loading framework to assess whether simulated quantum methods provide measurable advantages for pressurized water reactor fuel loading optimization.
  • Formulated the fuel reloading task as a constrained QUBO problem and benchmarked Random Search, Simulated Annealing, Simulated Quantum Annealing, Greedy Search, and Tabu Search.
  • Evaluated solver performance using feasibility rate, success probability, constraint satisfaction, objective quality, and time-to-solution across different core configurations.
  • Compared CNN- and GNN-based surrogate models for predicting core physics parameters including keff, Fa, and Fq using R2, RMSE, and parameter efficiency.
  • Integrated surrogate-assisted optimizers such as Genetic Algorithm, Iterated Local Search, Ant Colony Optimization, and Cross-Entropy search with QUBO-generated warm starts.
  • Found no clear simulated quantum advantage for the studied instances, while noting the limitation that physical quantum annealing hardware was not used.

Current Research

Quantum-Mechanical Study of the Electronic Properties of UxPuyOz Compounds

  • Conducted first-principles DFT simulations using Quantum ESPRESSO for uranium-based mixed oxides.
  • Calculated and benchmarked total energy, band gap, DOS/PDOS, stress, and force parameters.
  • Studied structure–property relationships to identify materials with promising mechanical and electronic performance.
  • Compared simulation outcomes with literature data for validation and benchmarking.
